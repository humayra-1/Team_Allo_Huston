**ABOUT THE PROJECT AND TEAM**

Lunaris is an interactive educational game designed to introduce players to the challenges and possibilities of human life on the Moon. Developed by Team Allo Houston for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, the project transforms complex space science concepts into an engaging and accessible learning experience, especially for young students, and beginner space enthusiasts. 

Through an astronaut's journey around a simulated lunar outpost, players explore essential facilities that support human survival and scientific research beyond Earth. These include Base Camp for communication and mission planning, Solar Arrays and a Nuclear Reactor for power generation, and the Power Management Center for energy distribution. Players also discover how the Greenhouse supports hydroponic food production, the Food Production Center manages supplies and waste recycling, and the Water Management Center ensures efficient water storage and reuse. The Scientific Laboratory enables research and experimentation, while the Crew Habitation Module provides a safe living environment for astronauts.

By exploring these interconnected systems, players can gain a better understanding of how science, engineering, and resource management could make sustainable lunar living possible. It simplifies complicated processes and systems into a playable game which makes the learning experience more enjoyable.

Our goal is to inspire curiosity, encourage scientific thinking, and make space exploration accessible through interactive learning and play.
